---
author: John Rawls
year: 1971
---
Rawls asks which principles of justice free and equal people would choose behind a veil of ignorance, not knowing their class, talents or conception of the good. From that original position he argues for equal basic liberties and for the difference principle: inequalities are just only when they work to the benefit of the least advantaged. Justice as fairness, against utilitarianism.
