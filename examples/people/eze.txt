# bread and garden
- Flour Water Salt Yeast, Ken Forkish: levain, hydration, long fermentation, dutch oven baking
- Tartine Bread, Chad Robertson: country loaf, starter, folds, bulk fermentation
- The Bread Baker's Apprentice, Peter Reinhart: preferments, shaping, proofing, crumb
- The One-Straw Revolution, Masanobu Fukuoka: natural farming, no tilling, rice and barley, seed balls
- Teaming with Microbes, Jeff Lowenfels: soil food web, compost, fungi and bacteria in the garden
- Gaia's Garden, Toby Hemenway: home scale permaculture, guilds, mulch, water
